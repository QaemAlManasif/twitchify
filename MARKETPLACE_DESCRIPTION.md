Twitchify brings Twitch to your CORSAIR XENEON EDGE, with live chat, one-tap moderation, an activity feed, stream stats, and broadcaster controls on the touchscreen next to your keyboard.

Watch any chat, moderate every channel you mod, and run your own stream without leaving your current game, app, or workspace.

What It Does

Twitchify connects to your Twitch account once, by approving a code on twitch.tv. No developer keys, no setup. Your channel and every channel you moderate are listed, plus the channels you follow that are live. Read chat, send messages, moderate, and keep an eye on follows, subs, cheers, and raids, all from the EDGE.

For Viewers

- Read the chat of any channel you follow, with the live ones listed first.

- Pin any channel to keep it in the list.

- Real Twitch badges and emotes, plus BTTV, 7TV, and FrankerFaceZ.

- Send messages and get emote suggestions as you type.

For Moderators

- Tap any message to delete it, time out, warn, or ban.

- Chat modes as quick actions: emote-only, followers-only, subscribers-only, unique messages, slow mode, and clear chat.

- Shield Mode, AutoMod queue, and a viewer list you can moderate from.

- Up to four chats side by side, with saved layouts.

- Works in every channel you moderate, with one sign-in.

For Streamers

- Activity feed: follows, subs, gift subs, cheers, raids, and the stream going live.

- Stats: live status with uptime, viewers, followers, subscribers, chat pace, and this session's totals.

- Stream info, clip, marker, ad break, and raid, as tiles or as a bar above the message box.

Widget Types

Add Twitchify to the screen more than once and give each copy a job from iCUE's widget settings:

- Everything

- Chat and controls

- Activity feed

- Quick actions

- Stats

- Custom panel

Copies share the sign-in and follow each other's channel switches. Each copy can have its own text size.

Custom Panels

Paste a link made to be embedded, the same kind you would use in an OBS browser source, and a copy of the widget shows it: alert boxes, goal bars, overlays.

Widget Settings

Every preference is available both in the widget and in iCUE's own settings panel for the placed widget, including layout, text size per area, colours, chat, and moderation options.

Watch Sync (Optional)

Watch Sync shows the chat of whatever Twitch tab you have open in your browser, and can follow the tab you are watching. It needs the Twitchify Watch Sync browser extension and a small helper app that runs with iCUE. Only channel names are shared, and only on your PC. The widget works fully without it.

Requirements

- CORSAIR iCUE 5.45 or newer with a XENEON EDGE.

- A Twitch account.

- Windows.

- Watch Sync (optional): the browser extension and the helper app. Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera, and other Chromium-based browsers use the Chrome extension. Firefox uses the Firefox add-on.
